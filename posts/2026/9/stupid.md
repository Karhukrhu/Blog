Lately I've been kinda upset. I mean it's nothing compared to what it used to be like few years ago, but it's still crippling. I guess this feeling has come to me bit by bit after a series of unpleasant events.

I feel like everything I do is just dumb. For example, updating this website rather than searching for a job. I feel like my website all in all is dumb and has no point. My "art" is just pretending to be something, there's no actual soul in it. There's no backstories, it's all just executing a task that I've given to myself.

Also, I have no idea how to behave in different situations so it makes me look like an idiot. I'm such bad at small talk and rather just sit there and say nothing. It still feels stupid. I have no initiative. Ohers do stuff around me and I'm just pondering what should I do in order not to look stupid. And I still end up looking stupid.

What also makes me upset is that I don't seem to achieve anything that is good for my health for instance. I don't like sports and I think I don't even like walking around where I live. It makes me uncomfortable so I just sit at home. There are so many things I should do for my wellbeing but none of them really inspire. 

There was an incidence when I was said to be too blunt and by that making people feel uncomfortable around me. And I had no idea that I was too blunt. I tried to be empahtetic, but instead I act bluntly and awkwadly. And I have no idea how to develop as a person since I don't even recognize how I'm behaving. I don't fit anywhere really. And this is not the first time I've been said so.

Even playing videogames make me often feel stupid. I don't have the patience to solve puzzles or think about compicated tactics that some games require. People I play with get frustrated with me 'cause I get frustrated by that game and myself. It is usually super hard for me to start a new game I've never played before because I assume I won't get it in the first place.

I will never ever ever take an IQ test! Well, to be honest, I don't think I even have the patience to complete one. What's funny is that my job coach encouraged everyone in my group to take the test, because it's "fun and interesting". And yes, I have an issue with that. 

My self-esteem has always been low, since like childhood. I have to constantly monitor how to behave and try my best so that people won't notice how imbecile I actually am. Constantly trying to fit in is exhausting and on top of that I should do something I don't like (like sports) and at the same time not to look stupid.

I know one would say I need to stop thinking what other people think about me because oftentimes they think nothing. Not to spend energy on contemplating irrelevant stuff. Easier said than done. 

I'm also foolish enough to publish this stupid blog post. Hope you enjoyed.
